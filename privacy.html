Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Overview

SiteRead is committed to protecting your privacy and your project data. This policy explains how we handle information when you use our service.

Data We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, company name, and billing information.

Project Documents

You upload construction specifications and project documents for analysis. These may contain proprietary project information.

Usage Data

We collect information about how you use SiteRead, including pages processed, features used, and error logs.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve our AI analysis services
  • To generate reports and submittal logs from your documents
  • To communicate with you about your account and service updates
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse

Data Security

We implement enterprise-grade security measures:

  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Documents are processed ephemerally and not stored permanently
  • We use secure cloud infrastructure with SOC 2 compliance
  • Access is restricted to authorized personnel only

AI Training

Important: We do not use your proprietary project data to train our AI models. Your specifications remain confidential and are only used to generate your specific reports.

Data Retention

Uploaded documents are automatically deleted after report generation is complete. Reports are retained for your access until you delete them or close your account.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications

Contact

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at privacy@siteread.app.

This privacy policy may be updated periodically. We will notify users of significant changes via email.
