About SiteRead

SiteRead was founded by a team of construction professionals and AI engineers who experienced firsthand the pain of reviewing thousands of pages of construction specifications.

We watched estimators spend 80+ hours manually scanning spec books, only to miss critical cost traps buried on page 847. We saw project engineers get blindsided by Delegated Design requirements they never knew existed.

So we built SiteRead - an AI-powered "second pair of eyes" that reads construction specifications the way humans do, but in minutes instead of days.
