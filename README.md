# SiteRead - Email Outreach Playbook

## What It Is
AI-powered scanner that finds hidden liability clauses (Delegated Design, Liquidated Damages, Sole Source) in construction spec PDFs before GCs bid.

**Offer**: "Send me a messy PDF → I send you a Risk Report."

---

## Target Audience

| Attribute | Criteria |
|-----------|----------|
| **Titles** | Senior Estimator, Estimator, Preconstruction Coordinator |
| **Company** | Commercial/ICI General Contractor (NOT residential) |
| **Size** | 50-500 employees (Tier 2/3) |
| **Location** | Toronto / GTA |

**LinkedIn Search**:
```
Title: "Estimator" OR "Preconstruction"
Industry: Construction
Headcount: 51-500
Location: Greater Toronto Area
```

---

## Email Script

**Subject**: Quick question about your spec review process

```
Hi [First Name],

Do you ever find yourself Ctrl+F'ing through 2,000-page spec books looking for Delegated Design clauses?

I'm a software developer in Toronto who built a tool that flags those automatically—Delegated Design, Liquidated Damages, Sole Source requirements.

I'm looking for 3 estimators to test it on real projects. I'll scan your messiest PDF for free and send you a Risk Report.

Worth a look?

– Mo
```

---

## Workflow

1. **Add leads** to `leads.csv` (Name, Company, Email)
2. **Run** `python3 send_emails.py`
3. **Confirm** each draft in terminal (Press `y`)

**Cadence**: 20 emails/week (Friday batch)
