FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my project data secure?

Yes. Security is our top priority. We process your specifications using enterprise-grade encryption. Files are analyzed ephemerally and are not stored after the report is generated. We do not use your proprietary project data to train our models.

Does this replace my Estimators?

No. SiteRead is designed to be a "Second Pair of Eyes." An Estimator's job is to build the project strategy; our job is to catch the boring, repetitive "gotchas" (like Delegated Design clauses) that are easy to miss at 2 AM. We handle the reading; you handle the strategy.

What specific risks do you flag?

We focus on Commercial & Liability Risks, not Engineering Code. Our algorithms specifically hunt for: Delegated Design requirements, Liquidated Damages clauses, Extended Warranty periods (beyond standard 1-year), and Sole Source manufacturers.

Do I need to upload special file formats?

No. Just upload the standard Project Spec Book (PDF). Whether it's a 200-page Architectural spec or a 2,000-page full Project Manual, our system can parse it.

We are currently in a Private Beta for select General Contractors in Toronto. During this period, we are running free pilot reports for qualified partners. Request access to see if you qualify.
